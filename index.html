Privacy

Your data never leaves your device. Everyone you add, every conversation, every photo and memory — all of it lives only in your browser's local storage. There is no Cognom server, no database, no account.

AI provider. When you record a conversation or thought, the audio (or text) is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose in Settings — Google Gemini or OpenAI. Cognom doesn't see or store it. You provide your own API key and pay (or use the free tier) directly with them.

What this means in practice. Clear your browser's site data and your Cognom is gone. Use a different device and you start fresh there. Use the Backup button in the sidebar regularly to save a file of your data somewhere safe.

Recordings. Audio is processed in memory and discarded after analysis. Only the AI's written notes about the conversation are saved.
